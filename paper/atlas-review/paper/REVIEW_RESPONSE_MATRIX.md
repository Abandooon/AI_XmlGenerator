# Response to the editor and reviewers

Dear Editor and Reviewers,

Thank you for your assessment of the manuscript. We have revised the paper to present a metamodel-guided method for model generation with layered constraints. The explanation now begins with the modeling problem and a periodic-event example, defines the ICM and the two constraint layers consistently, and separates the general method from the resources and tools required for each domain adaptation.

The revised evaluation reports the provenance and scope of the current AUTOSAR component tasks, observes decoding interventions in a separate local AUTOSAR experiment, and adds railway model repair and structured private-international-law decisions. It distinguishes task acceptance from broader domain coverage and reports successful repairs, remaining failures, repeated-run outcomes and recorded model use. The current AUTOSAR results replace the earlier multi-file evaluation; they use the tasks, artifact scope and checking backends documented in this revision and are not combined with the earlier results.

Some requests are addressed by additional evidence, while others require limits on the claims. We did not measure person-hours for preparation, review or human takeover, and we did not conduct a practitioner study of productivity, usability or confidence. These parts of the requested empirical evaluation remain unmeasured. The response and manuscript state this limitation explicitly; model tokens and response times are not substitutes for human effort.

The 31 stable response identifiers below organize related concerns. They are paraphrases and may split a long original paragraph or cover overlapping comments. Each source entry links to the relevant lines of the [decision letter](https://github.com/Abandooon/AI_XmlGenerator/blob/ATLAS/paper/sources/SOSYM-26-00005681_DECISION_LETTER_2026-06-18.txt). The [comment source map](https://github.com/Abandooon/AI_XmlGenerator/blob/ATLAS/paper/COMMENT_SOURCE_MAP.md) records the original passages and their relationship to these responses, including the requests about abstract motivation and the reason for combining existing capabilities.

All section, figure, table and appendix references below refer to the [revised English manuscript](https://github.com/Abandooon/AI_XmlGenerator/blob/ATLAS/paper/current/english/main.pdf), available with its [LaTeX source](https://github.com/Abandooon/AI_XmlGenerator/blob/ATLAS/paper/current/english/main.tex) and [compiled location index](https://github.com/Abandooon/AI_XmlGenerator/blob/ATLAS/paper/current/english/LOCATIONS.md). Section 3 presents the method. Sections 4.2–4.5 and Appendices A–D cover AUTOSAR, local AUTOSAR-vLLM, railway models and private international law in the same order. The [artifact crosswalk](https://github.com/Abandooon/AI_XmlGenerator/blob/ATLAS/docs/PAPER_ARTIFACT_CROSSWALK.md) connects these locations to the data, code and offline verification procedures. The main paired comparisons remain in Appendices C.2 and D.2; complete analyses and sensitivity checks are linked under [S].
